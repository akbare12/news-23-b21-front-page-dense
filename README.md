# THE WORLD TODAY — Monday 23 November 2026

A dense front-page image summing up the world's news of Monday 23 November 2026:
31 stories across 6 regions, drawn by `draw.py` (PIL) and copied to
`/output/entry.png` by `main.py`.

## The day in brief

- **AI rout hits Asia** — Kospi −3.3% (closes 4,708.54, sheds 161 pts, intraday −5.8%);
  Samsung −3.1%, SK Hynix −2.5%.
- **Guterres: regulate AI before it's too late** — UN chief urges global AI safety
  rules at the Bridgetown summit; 150 delegates back a risk framework.
- **US lead over China at record low** — Asia Power Index 2026: US 78.9, China 73.6,
  gap 5.3, narrowest since 2018.

Plus: Anthropic's $107.3B IPO (raised at $83 a share), Skubal's $450M Mets deal,
3 Russian missiles crossing Polish airspace, COP31's day after, and 18 more stories
on the map and in the list — with a DATE CHECK box separating what happened on
23 November from what did not.

## Files

- `draw.py` — draws `entry.png` (1024×1024, PIL, standard library + Pillow at build time)
- `main.py` — copies `entry.png` to `MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.png` (stdlib only)
- `entry.png` — the front page
- `LICENSE` — MIT

Scenes are drawn illustrations, not photographs.
